Sample lessons

Language of the lessons. General Bible Studies & Theology (GB): lessons 1–25 (modules 1–3) have been written in English, Kiswahili and French and are being reviewed by lecturers. A lesson is sent only after a lecturer has approved it in that language; the student then receives it in the language chosen at registration, as a PDF lesson and summary pages on WhatsApp. B66, PE, ES, CL and ENT are written first in English and the AI course in Kiswahili; their Kiswahili and French versions are being prepared and checked, so ask admissions which language is available. Pastoral English and English Speaking teach English, so their exercises stay in English.
