AI study assistant

An assistant for your studies that uses only the school's materials.

Not yet open. This service has been built and tested in a test environment; it opens only when the school approves the launch. Until then, ask on WhatsApp. Ask on WhatsApp

What it does

  • Explains a lesson you have access to.
  • Guides you through an exercise with hints, without giving the answers.
  • Helps with your AI course project: report, content, video, audio or website.
  • Helps lecturers draft a book outline.

Its rules

  • It answers only from materials the school allows, and names its source.
  • When the materials do not answer your question, it says so.
  • It never invents marks, payments, approvals or progress: those come from the school's records.

Cost and limits

Each question is one request to an AI model, and there is a daily limit of requests. The cost for students and the number of requests per day will be announced before the service opens.
