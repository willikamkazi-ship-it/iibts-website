How a lesson works

  1. You receive the lesson as a PDF on WhatsApp.
  2. You follow the study plan: reading, Scripture or examples, and exercises.
  3. You send your task to your tutor on WhatsApp (text, or a voice message for the English courses).
  4. After sending your task, you open the answer key and receive feedback.
