Have your personal student link?

Paste the personal link sent to your WhatsApp. It opens your existing account; you do not need to register again. An expired link must be renewed through OBTS WhatsApp.

Your link is checked on this device and is not saved by this website. Keep it private.

A personal link is sent to your own WhatsApp number. The link works for a limited time; ask for a new one when it expires.
