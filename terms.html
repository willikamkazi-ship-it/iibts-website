Terms of use

The conditions for using this website and OBTS materials.

Purpose

This website gives information about the Online Bible & Theology School (OBTS) and lets you contact admissions.

Course materials

Lessons and other materials sent to students are for the student's personal study. Please do not publish or sell them without written permission from OBTS.

Information on this website

OBTS works to keep this information correct. Fees, dates, certificates and other details are valid only when OBTS announces them; anything not yet announced is shown as "to be announced".

Draft materials

Draft lessons, answer keys and lecturer guides are not published on this website and are not sent to students before they are approved.

Respect in learning groups

Students are asked to communicate respectfully with tutors and other students, and to protect other people's personal information.
