Frequently asked questions

Short answers to common questions.

Who can study at OBTS?

Anyone who wants to learn and can follow lessons on a phone. Pastoral English starts from a basic level of English (A2); English Speaking starts with a placement recording.

Do I need a computer?

No. A phone with WhatsApp and a PDF reader is enough.

How much are the fees, and when do courses start?

Fees and start dates have not yet been announced. Ask on WhatsApp and admissions will tell you when they are confirmed.

Can I take more than one course?

Admissions will confirm how many courses you can take at the same time.

In which language are the lessons?

General Bible Studies & Theology: the first 25 lessons have been written in English, Kiswahili and French and are being reviewed by lecturers; once approved, you receive them in the language you choose when you register. The other courses are written first in English, and the AI course in Kiswahili; their Kiswahili and French versions are being prepared and reviewed, and admissions will tell you what is available.

Will I receive a certificate?

Information about certificates will be given only when it has been approved. Please do not rely on any promise that is not on this website or from OBTS admissions.

How are my details used?

See the privacy policy. The registration form on this website stores nothing; it only prepares a WhatsApp message.
