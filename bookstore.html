Before a book is sold

  1. The book goes through idea, outline, chapters, sources, review, cover and PDF, and is approved.
  2. The school approves the sample you may read before buying.
  3. The school approves the price.

How you will buy

  1. Read the book's page: description, sample and approved price.
  2. Order with your WhatsApp number; you receive an order number and the amount.
  3. Pay with the payment method announced by OBTS and send the transaction number.
  4. A member of the finance staff verifies the payment; then the book is sent to you and the delivery is recorded.
